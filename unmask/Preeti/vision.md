# Preeti — Unmask Vision

## Brand name = brand promise

**Unmask** means being **real, raw, authentic**. The mask comes off in conversation — not in packaging.

## Who it must reach

- People of **all age groups**
- Anyone who relates to **friendships** and **relationships**
- **Different people** — different lives, same feelings
- People **all around the world**

## How it should feel

- Couch-level honesty, not a performance
- Room for laughter and tears in the same sitting
- Organic talk over overproduced polish
