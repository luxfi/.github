# Unmask — Content Pillars & Short-Form Strategy

**Starts:** 15 August  
**Brand:** Real. Raw. Authentic.  
**Edit rule:** Conversation first → packaging later.

---

## Positioning

Unmask is a couch-level, human conversation show. People of every age should see themselves in it — friendships, relationships, and the messy middle of being real with someone else.

**Inspiration qualities (not copies):**
- Mel Robbins → clear, useful information
- Drew Barrymore → real people; cry and laugh in the same sitting

---

## Content pillars

Use these pillars for guest booking, episode framing, and clip selection.

### 1. Unmasked stories
Personal turning points, admissions, “I never said this out loud.”  
*Why:* Core brand — remove the mask.

### 2. Friendships & relationships
How we love, leave, repair, and stay. Friends, partners, family, found family.  
*Why:* Preeti’s explicit relatability themes.

### 3. Different people, same feelings
Guests from different worlds (podcasters, personalities, “regular” people, global voices). Same emotions, different lives.  
*Why:* All ages + worldwide relatability.

### 4. Useful honesty (Mel-mode)
Practical takeaways without losing warmth — what actually helped, what they’d tell their younger self.  
*Why:* Information podcaster quality Preeti wants.

### 5. Couch moments (Drew-mode)
Laugh, silence, tears, looks between hosts/guests — the organic beats Sharad wants preserved.  
*Why:* Emotion and presence over polish.

Pillars 1–3 drive **booking**. Pillars 4–5 drive **edit and clip selection**.

## Formats

| Format | Length | Notes |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Long-form conversation | ~30–40 min source | 3 camera angles; organic talk |
| Full episode cut | Edit-led; packaging light | Remove fat; keep funny / human moments |
| Short-form | 15–60s (platform-fit) | YouTube-style broader clips; not overcut |

### Shoot defaults (Sharad)

- 3 angles  
- 30–40 minutes  
- Simple setup, easy shoot  
- Capture looks, pauses, self-answered questions, humour  
- Packaging deferred until the edit works  

---

## Short-form strategy (from Aug 15)

### Goals
1. Prove Unmask tone in-feed (real > slick).
2. Feed discovery for long-form.
3. Build a reusable clip grammar so every shoot yields shorts without extra days.

### Clip types (map to pillars)

| Clip type | Pillar | Example hook |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Confession cut | Unmasked stories | “I never told anyone this…” |
| Relationship beat | Friendships & relationships | “The moment I knew we’d changed…” |
| Contrast guest | Different people | Unexpected guest + universal feeling |
| One useful line | Useful honesty | Clear advice in one breath |
| Look / laugh / silence | Couch moments | Exchange of looks; Preeti answering herself |

### Cadence (starter — adjust after 2 weeks)

- **3–5 shorts / week** from existing or new shoots  
- Mix: ~40% emotion, ~30% useful, ~30% humour / organic  
- Platforms: lead where the audience already is; keep masters vertical + horizontal from the 3-angle shoot  

### Guest / talent pipeline (Preeti)

Prioritise for short-form *and* episodes:
1. Podcasters  
2. Personalities  
3. General / unexpected voices  

Each guest brief should name **one primary pillar** and **2–3 clip moments** to watch for in the room.

---

## Workflow

```text
Book (pillar) → Shoot (3 angles, 30–40m, easy) → Edit conversation
        → Pull shorts (clip types) → Light packaging last
```

### Packaging later means
- No heavy motion / animation blocking the first cut  
- Reuse branding/animation kit once commercials are locked  
- Fundamentals refined once; not reinvented per episode  

---

## Success signals (first cycle after Aug 15)

- Shorts feel unmistakably Unmask without a logo dump  
- At least one clip per episode from “couch moments”  
- Guests span more than one demographic / world  
- Team spends more time editing conversation than decorating it
